# GFX-09: night windows

Windows and fire are the bright pixels at night. Circular ground halos
are gone.

- Emission **#F2B56B** on a small pane, not a glowing box
- One **shadowless OmniLight3D** per inhabited building
- Short range (3.2–4.2 m), low energy (0.38–0.58), distance fade
- No daytime window emission

Stacked on GFX-08. No HUD, audio, gameplay or workflow changes.
