# Game Pillars

## 1. Living Logistics

The world should feel alive because resources move physically through it. Workers, storage, production, and transport are visible.

## 2. Settlement Before Empire

The first experience is not conquest. It is making a small settlement function.

## 3. Simulation First

The game should be driven by clear systems: resources, buildings, workers, tasks, paths, storage, production, and persistence.

## 4. Player as Founder

The player should feel like the founder of a domain. They place infrastructure and watch the settlement respond.

## 5. Readable Isometric World

The world should be easy to understand visually. The player should always know what workers are doing and where resources are going.
