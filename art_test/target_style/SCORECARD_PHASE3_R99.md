# SCORECARD — Phase 3 R99 (round174): leaf-built crowns (internal only)

## My choice: stay on trees — leaf-built silhouette
The named R98 remainder and a matter of kind. R98's smooth shading, warm/cool
clusters, cluster texture, and R69 discipline (count, spacing, scale,
contact shadows, no near-black) are all kept; sub-masses are per-tree form,
not more trees.

## Leaf-built crowns — 6.5/10
- **Scalloped silhouette**: canopy displacement gained a high-frequency
  three-sine product term (16/14/18, ±0.17) on top of R98's multi-scale
  displacement, so sphere outlines break into bumps instead of smooth arcs.
- **Cluster separation**: every 4th broadleaf sub-mass is darkened 20%
  (shadow-side clusters), against the existing lightened sun clusters —
  internal tone gaps between sub-masses at crown scale.
- **Mini lobes**: 7 small canopy lobes (0.30-0.43 scale) placed around each
  broadleaf crown's perimeter with varied tone (light top / dark side / mid),
  making the crown outline read as overlapping leaf clusters rather than one
  displaced sphere per cluster.
**Do crowns read leaf-built or still modelled blobs at full frame? Between,
leaning leaf-built.** Crown outlines are now lobed/scalloped and cluster
separation reads at full frame (compare R98 reference panel); the close still
shows the construction — smooth-shaded sub-masses assembled into a crown —
where the target's crowns are painted leaf masses with finer internal break-up
that this geometry cannot reach without fine detail (which mip-averages, R66).

**Overall honest self-score ~6.5/10. Not a Black PASS claim.**
