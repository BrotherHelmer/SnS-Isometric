## Identification (honest process note)
The "dark pillar cluster beside the mine" was NOT the crest rocks — two diagnostic
renders (crest row disabled: no change; all hill-functions disabled: pillars gone)
isolated them to `_summit()`: a standing-stone ring at (9.0,-8.8) — five 3.1-3.8-tall
dark grey (59564f) stones with white eef2f4 caps plus a 3.4-tall centre pillar with

grey pillars with snow caps, taller than the mine frame beside them.

## Fix — geometry, not tint
- Standing stones: height 3.1-3.8 -> 1.15-1.65, widened (0.62->0.78), bases sunk
  0.38 into the slope, colour warmed 59564f -> 7d6a52, caps shrunk and warmed
  (eef2f4 -> d8cbb2, seated on the new tops). Centre pillar: 3.4 -> 1.7 tall, sunk,
  same warming; its pennant lowered with it (still marks the summit, now low).
- Supporting: crest rocks (cri row) and hill boulders (si9/ti9/li2) flattened and
  sunk into `_h` (scaleY ~0.4-0.55, bases at terrain), crest snow reseated — the
  whole crest silhouette is now low humps, without flattening it entirely.
- Mine untouched: position (14.5,-11.5), 1.7x warm timber frame + wheel kept.
  Snow patches seated on `_h` terrain (not on the lowered rocks) — unaffected,
  verified in both renders.
